Hearing check

The one question the whole project hangs on: can the browser understand the speech of a four-year-old? It takes an evening to answer — before writing any more of the game.

How to run it

Press the button and let the child say ten commands in their own words, without dictating. For example: "take the red ball", "give me the small cube", "put the ball in the box".
